开始使用 / Getting started

AcademicUpdater 1.2.0 · 本地个人研究资料库 / Local personal research library

中文

研究范围、来源与计划、AI 连接现在统一放在“个人中心”。

  1. 双击应用文件夹中的 Start.cmd 启动，在浏览器打开 http://127.0.0.1:8765。关闭浏览器不会停止后台采集；双击 Stop.cmd 停止应用。
  2. 在“AI 连接”选择 ChatGPT 登录、单独计费的 API，或本地兼容模型。完成连接后获取模型列表、选择文本模型并保存。ChatGPT 订阅调用取决于账号是否授予官方登录权限；未获授权时请手动选择其他方式。应用不会自动改用付费 API。
  3. 在“研究范围”写下你的兴趣，让 AI 展示理解后的领域、研究问题、关键词、排除项和不明确边界。修改理解后点击“应用此研究范围”。未连接 AI 时，可手动定义范围并开始真实采集。
  4. 在“来源与计划”设置 Crossref、arXiv 和公开 RSS／Atom 订阅。行业动态和会议报告通过你添加的对应类别订阅进入资料库。点击“采集最新内容”开始；也可直接导入 DOI、arXiv 编号、论文或资讯页面。
  5. 在资料库收藏、标记已读、写笔记、查看来源版本及 AI 分析依据。界面可切换中英文，摘要语言可单独设置，原始标题、描述和笔记始终保留。

推理、排序及期刊指标

“AI 连接”中选择模型支持的推理强度，例如高或超高，并保存设置。设置用于新任务，恢复的批量任务保留原来的模型、强度及研究范围；已有结果保留，可在论文详情重新分析单篇。

今日更新按相关性排列，并以颜色和文字区分等级。资料库支持第一作者姓氏、期刊、相关性、IF 等排序；筛选立即生效，搜索会自动刷新。后台任务期间约每两秒显示新进度。

论文详情可录入期刊 IF、指标年份和来源，资料库提供 CSV 模板和批量导入。必填列为 journal、year、impact_factor、source，可选 issns（分号分隔）、source_url。数值标注为用户提供，同一期刊的新旧论文都会显示最新年份数据。暂无数值不会猜测，预印本等显示不适用。

暂停与继续

任务逐篇保存。点击暂停会先完成当前论文；点击继续处理剩余内容。应用意外停止后，重新启动会自动恢复中断的采集／分析任务。手动暂停或额度用尽后需点击继续。强制停止时未保存的当前请求可能重新发送，已保存的分析会跳过。

研究发展史

在新栏目输入领域，让 AI 展示理解后的研究范围及检索词，也可以手动填写。年份留空可检索全部历史年份；选择来源、条目上限及是否使用 AI 后，点击“保存并开始检索”。已保存检索保留配置、论文、相关性评估和带论文引用的时间线，可再次打开或导出。

“全部可取得的匹配结果”仍受来源收录、检索词和访问上限影响，不保证找全整个领域。覆盖详情显示匹配数、已取回数、限制和来源错误。论文逐批分析，发展概述最多引用 80 篇跨年份选取的相关文献；所有论文仍保留。仅由历史检索发现的论文不会进入每日更新，已有资料会自动去重。任务支持暂停、继续和重启恢复。

数据与运行

数据位于应用旁的 data/academicupdater.sqlite3，每次采集前备份至 data/backups（每日一个文件）。资料库可导出 JSONL、CSV 或数据库备份。凭据单独存储为 data/credentials.dpapi，由当前 Windows 账号加密，数据库导出不包含凭据。

定时采集需要应用运行、电脑唤醒并能联网。默认每天 08:00（UTC+10），下次启动会检查遗漏更新；没有安装系统开机启动任务。恢复备份时先停止应用，把现有 data 文件夹改名保留，新建 data 文件夹，将备份复制为 academicupdater.sqlite3，再启动并重新连接 AI。

当前边界

当前分析基于来源标题及摘要／描述，不代表已读取论文全文。没有摘要的条目会明确标示。Crossref 的索引更新可能包含旧论文；本应用区分来源发布日期与首次发现时间。查询结果有上限，达到上限会提示，不声称覆盖所有文献。确定去重使用 DOI、arXiv 编号、来源编号和链接；没有共同标识的相似标题暂不自动合并。更换研究范围会保留原资料并重新评估，旧摘要保存在历史中。

AI 次数上限包含失败尝试，限制请求次数而非费用。云端分析会传输研究范围及资料标题／摘要；笔记保存在本机。账户授权及实际推理需在你的账号连接后验证。新机器需要 Python 3.11+，ChatGPT 身份验证还需安装 requirements.txt 中的 cryptography 依赖。

English

Research field, Sources & schedule and AI connection are now grouped under Personal profile.

  1. Double-click Start.cmd, then open http://127.0.0.1:8765. Closing the browser keeps the background application running. Use Stop.cmd to stop it.
  2. In AI connection, choose official ChatGPT sign-in, the separately billed OpenAI API, or a local compatible model. Load the available models, select a structured-output text model, and save. ChatGPT plan access requires an eligible account and explicit authorization. There is no automatic fallback to paid API usage.
  3. Describe your interests in Research field. Review and edit the interpreted discipline, question, keywords, exclusions, and ambiguities, then apply the scope. You can also define a scope manually and collect real records before connecting AI.
  4. Configure Crossref, arXiv, and public RSS/Atom feeds in Sources & schedule. Add industry and conference feeds with the corresponding content type. Collect updates or import a DOI, arXiv identifier, publication page, or news URL.
  5. Save items, track reading, write notes, inspect source versions, and review grounded AI excerpts. Interface and summary languages are independent; original content is preserved.

Reasoning, sorting and journal metrics

Select supported reasoning effort in AI connection and save. New tasks use these settings; resumed batches keep their original model, effort and research scope. Prior analyses remain available; reanalyze individual papers from their details.

Today ranks by relevance with colors and text. Library supports sorting by first-author surname, journal, relevance, IF and dates. Filters apply immediately, typing searches automatically, and active tasks refresh approximately every two seconds.

Enter a journal IF, metric year and source in paper details, or download the CSV template and import in Library. Required columns: journal, year, impact_factor, source. Optional: issns (semicolon separated), source_url. Metrics are user supplied, with the latest year shown for both old and new matching papers. Missing values are not inferred; preprints and other inapplicable records are labeled accordingly.

Pause and resume

Each completed paper is saved. Pause lets the current paper finish, and Resume processes remaining work. Restart automatically resumes interrupted collection/analysis. Manual and quota pauses require Resume. Forced termination may retry the unsaved in-flight request; saved analyses are skipped.

Research history

Describe an area, review AI’s interpreted field and queries, or enter them manually. Leave years blank for the available publication history. Choose sources, candidate limits and optional AI assessment, then Save and search. Saved searches retain their scope, papers, assessments and cited timeline, and can be reopened or exported.

All available matches remains subject to indexing, query wording and access limits; it does not guarantee exhaustive field coverage. Coverage details show counts, caps and source errors. AI assesses papers in batches; its overview samples up to 80 relevant papers across publication years. All papers remain saved. Historical-only discoveries stay out of Today, and existing items are deduplicated. Pause, resume and restart recovery preserve progress.

Data and operation

The SQLite library is stored in data/academicupdater.sqlite3. Collection creates a daily backup in data/backups. Export the complete library as JSONL, CSV, or SQLite. Credentials are stored separately in data/credentials.dpapi, encrypted for the current Windows account, and are excluded from database exports.

The daily scheduler requires this application to be running and the computer awake and online. It defaults to 08:00 at a fixed UTC+10 offset and checks missed updates after restarting. No operating-system startup task is installed. To restore a backup, stop the application, rename the existing data folder to keep it safe, create a new data folder, copy the backup as academicupdater.sqlite3, restart, and reconnect AI.

Current boundaries

Analysis uses titles and source abstracts/descriptions, not full papers. Crossref index updates can include older publications; publication dates and first discovery dates are separate. Source result caps are explicit and do not imply exhaustive coverage. Deduplication uses DOI, arXiv ID, source ID, and canonical URL. Similar titles without shared identifiers are not automatically merged. Scope changes retain the library and prior analyses.

The daily AI limit counts attempted requests, not spending. Cloud analysis sends the research scope and source title/abstract; notes stay local. Account authorization and live inference must be verified with your connected account. On another machine install Python 3.11+ and the cryptography dependency in requirements.txt for ChatGPT identity verification.
